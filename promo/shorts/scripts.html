Hegemarch Shorts Scripts

Five vertical Shorts (1080×1920) for YouTube, with a narration script for each, timed to the cuts. The music in the videos is a quiet bed with no melody, so the voice can sit on top. Each line is written to fit its shot at a relaxed pace, about two and a half words a second.

Saying the name

Suggested: HEJ-uh-march, as in "hegemony". Say the website as "hegemarch dot com".

Tone

A storyteller, not an announcer: warm, a little wry, unhurried. Let the pauses marked in italics breathe; the picture fills them.

Mixing

The videos come out at −20 LUFS. Record the voice dry and mix it about 6 dB above the bed, for −14 LUFS overall: YouTube's level.
