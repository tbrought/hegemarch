The Six Houses Scripts

A lore series of six vertical Shorts (1080×1920), one house a week, with a narration script for each, timed to the cuts. Every episode has the same shape: the house's Ark firing its ability (no words), its name, where it comes from, how it plays, its Ark, its motto, and next week's house. Each line fits its shot at about two and a half words a second.

Saying the names

HEJ-uh-march, as in "hegemony"; the website is "hegemarch dot com".

VAIL · VAIL-or · lie-SEE-um · lie-SEE-uh · KAN-tih-kul · aw-REE-lee-uh

Tone

A storyteller, not an announcer. The hook is said over the slowed opening; the motto is said slowly, over the night, and is the last thing before the sign-off. Let the beats in italics breathe.

Mixing

The videos come out at −20 LUFS with a bed of each house's own tune and no melody. Record the voice dry, mix it about 6 dB above the bed, and master to −14 LUFS: YouTube's level.

Playlist description

Six houses fight to crown the next Hegemarch and light the dark sector again. Each week, one Short tells the story of one house: where it comes from, how it plays, and the Ark it sends to war. Which house are you? Play Hegemarch free in your browser: hegemarch.com

Posting

One a week, in this order, the same day each week, to YouTube Shorts, TikTok and Reels. Title each "The [House] · The Six Houses · Hegemarch". Pin a comment: Which house are you?
