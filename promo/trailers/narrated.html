The Epic trailer, cut to be narrated by the developer. The story runs as before, from the Stone Age to the stars, but each shot is long enough for its line, the story's on-screen sentences are now a keyword or two, and the music is a quiet bed with no melody so the voice sits on top.

Pace

About two and a half words a second, an easy read. A line can run a beat into the next shot; only the Starfall lines need to land on cue.

Saying the name

HEJ-uh-march, as in "hegemony", the same as in the Shorts. Say the website as "hegemarch dot com".

Mixing

The video comes out at −20 LUFS. Record the voice dry and mix it about 6 dB above the bed, for −14 LUFS overall: YouTube's level.
