      ONE RUN, START TO CROWN

      An 8-bit real-time space strategy roguelite. Every run is a new sector, with new rivals and new Boons; a medium one takes about 35 to 50 minutes. What you saw above is the game itself.

      1. KINDLE

        Send a seed ship to a dark world. It floods with your color and starts to pay you Embers.

      2. BUILD

        Forges turn Embers into warships. Send them down the starlanes, and pause whenever you want to think.

      3. DRAFT

        Lore fills as you play. Each time it does, choose one Boon of three, and the run bends your way.

      4. CROWN

        Fulfil a Destiny before a rival house does, and you are the next Hegemarch.

      SIX HOUSES, ONE CROWN

      Three houses are yours from the first run. Three more are earned with Legacy, which every run pays, won or lost. Choose one here and the page takes its color.

      THREE SHIPS, ONE TRIANGLE

      Every fleet starts from three designs. Each beats one and loses to another, so what you build should answer what you see.

            OR BEGIN IN THE STONE AGE

            An Ages run is the long game. Every house begins as a tribe on one cradle world, climbs five ages, builds its Ark, and carries what it has won to the stars, where the crown is decided.

            • 50 to 110 minutes from the first fire to the crown
            • A Boon with every age, and every unit you have is upgraded to it
            • What you win on the cradle goes with your Ark
