HEGEMARCH

Light up a dark sector, world by world.

An 8-bit real-time space strategy roguelite. Kindle dark worlds, raise a fleet, choose Boons that bend the rules, and follow one of three Destinies to the crown. Every run is a new sector, with new rivals and new Boons.
